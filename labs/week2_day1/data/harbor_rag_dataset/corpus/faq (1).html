Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the questions members ask us most. Can't find what you need? Contact us and we'll be happy to help.

How do I become a member of Harbor Credit Union?

Anyone who lives, works, worships or attends school in our service area, and their immediate family members, can join. A minimum deposit of $5.00 in a Primary Share Account establishes your membership. You can open your membership online in about ten minutes or at any branch.

What are the Member Contact Centre hours?

Our Member Contact Centre is available Monday through Friday from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. and Saturday from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time. You can reach us at 1-800-4-HARBOR. To report a lost or stolen card outside these hours, choose option 1 at any time and your call will be routed to our 24-hour card service.

How do I order checks?

You can reorder checks in online banking under Account Services, or call the Member Contact Centre. Your first box of Harbor standard checks is free with Premier Checking.

When will my direct deposit be available?

Payroll and government direct deposits are made available as soon as Harbor receives the payment file, which may be up to two days before the scheduled payment date. Early availability depends on when the payer sends the file and is not guaranteed.

How do I set up e-statements?

Sign in to online banking, open the Documents tab, and select Go Paperless. You will receive an e-mail each month when your statement is ready. Members who receive e-statements are not charged the paper statement fee.

What should I do if I see a charge I don't recognize?

First check with anyone else who uses the card, and look for a merchant name that may differ from the store name. If you still don't recognize the charge, contact us right away so we can review it and, if needed, start a dispute on your behalf.

Can I get my account and routing numbers online?

Yes. In online banking or the mobile app, select the account and choose Account Details. For your security, the full account number is shown only after you confirm a one-time passcode.

Do you charge a fee to close an account?

There is no fee to close an account unless it is closed within 90 days of opening. Please see our Schedule of Fees and Charges for details.

How do I update my address or phone number?

You can update your mailing address, e-mail and phone number in online banking under Profile. Changes to your phone number trigger a verification code to the new number and a notice to your previous e-mail address.

Is my money insured?

Yes. Deposits at Harbor are federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration up to at least $250,000 per member, per ownership category.

Can I make a loan payment from another financial institution?

Yes. You can make a one-time or recurring payment from an external account in online banking under Transfers, or mail a check to our loan payment address shown on your statement.

How do I apply for a loan?

Apply in the mobile app, in online banking, by phone, or at any branch. Most applications receive a decision within one business day. Auto loan rates are shown on our Auto Loan Product Comparison sheet.
