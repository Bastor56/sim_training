# Share Certificate Account Disclosure

_Disclosure form DEP-DSC-017 | Effective: July 1, 2026 | This disclosure supplements the Savings Account Terms and Conditions._

## Rate Information

The dividend rate and Annual Percentage Yield (APY) for your Share Certificate are set on the date the certificate is opened and are fixed for the term. Current rates are published on the Deposit Rate Sheet and are available at any branch. The APY assumes dividends remain on deposit until maturity; a withdrawal of dividends will reduce earnings.

## Compounding and Crediting

Dividends are compounded daily and credited monthly. You may elect to have dividends credited to the certificate, transferred to your Primary Share Account, or transferred to a Harbor checking account. The dividend period is monthly, beginning on the first calendar day of the month and ending on the last calendar day of the month.

## Minimum Balance Requirements

The minimum deposit to open a Share Certificate is $1,000. You must maintain a balance of at least $1,000 in the certificate for the full term to obtain the disclosed APY. Balance tiers are determined by the opening deposit.

## Transaction Limitations

After the certificate is opened, you may not make additional deposits until maturity. Withdrawals of principal before maturity are permitted only as a full withdrawal and are subject to the early withdrawal penalty described below.

## Early Withdrawal Penalty

If you withdraw principal before the maturity date, a penalty equal to 90 days of dividends applies to certificates with terms of 12 months or less. For certificates with terms longer than 12 months, a penalty equal to 180 days of dividends applies. The penalty is calculated on the amount withdrawn at the dividend rate in effect on the certificate, and it may reduce principal if dividends earned are less than the penalty.

The penalty is waived when a withdrawal is made because an owner has died or has been declared legally incompetent by a court.

## Renewal and Grace Period

Certificates renew automatically at maturity for the same term at the rate then in effect. You have a grace period of 10 calendar days after maturity to withdraw funds or change the term without penalty. We will send you a maturity notice at least 30 days before a certificate of more than 12 months matures.

## Adding Owners and Beneficiaries

You may add a joint owner or name payable-on-death beneficiaries when the certificate is opened or at renewal. Changes to ownership during the term may require the certificate to be closed and reopened, which would be treated as an early withdrawal. Individual Retirement Account (IRA) certificates are subject to additional terms in the IRA disclosure.

## Insurance

Member accounts are federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration up to at least $250,000 per member, per ownership category.
