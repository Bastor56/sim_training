SUPERVISOR NOTE (posted 08/12/2026, updated 08/19/2026)
Known issue: in the mobile app, bill pay returns error BP-503 when a member schedules a payment more than 30 days in the future. The fix ships in app release 7.14.
Workaround for error BP-503: have the member schedule the payment from the desktop online banking site, which is not affected by the error.
Do not advise members to delete and reinstall the app; it does not clear the error. Log each occurrence with wrap-up code DIG-BP503.
