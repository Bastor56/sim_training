Member Newsletter of Harbor Credit Union | Summer 2026

Carrow Bay Branch Opens This Fall

We are excited to announce that our newest location, the Carrow Bay branch at 88 Wharf Street, is nearly finished. After eighteen months of construction and a lot of input from members who live and work on the east side of the bay, the Carrow Bay branch will open to members on Monday, October 19, 2026. The branch will offer a drive-up lane, a 24-hour ATM that accepts deposits, and two private offices for loan and financial wellness appointments.

Join us for an open house during the first week, with coffee from local roasters and a chance to meet the branch team.

Last Call for the Summer Saver Certificate

Members have opened more than 2,400 Summer Saver certificates since June. If you have been thinking about locking in a rate, there is still time. The Summer Saver 13-month certificate at 4.60% APY is available through September 30, 2026, with a minimum opening deposit of $1,000. After that date, standard certificate rates from the Deposit Rate Sheet will apply to new certificates.

Annual Meeting and Board Election

Thank you to the more than 3,100 members who voted in this year's board election. The Annual Meeting was held in May at the Port Alden Community Hall, where members heard reports on the credit union's growth, its capital position and new investments in digital banking. Minutes of the meeting are available on request from the Board Secretary.

Protect Yourself from Impersonation Scams

Scammers increasingly pretend to be credit union staff. Remember that Harbor will never ask you to move money to a "safe account", never ask for your one-time passcode, and never ask you to send a wire to protect your savings. If someone calls claiming to be from Harbor and asks for any of these things, hang up and call us using the number on the back of your card.

You can also lock your debit card instantly in the Harbor mobile app while you investigate.

Community Corner

This summer Harbor employees volunteered more than 900 hours with food banks, youth sports leagues and shoreline clean-ups across our service area. Our Financial Wellness team also delivered free budgeting workshops at four public libraries. Look for the fall workshop schedule in next month's e-mail update.

Digital Banking Upgrade

Later this year we will upgrade online banking with a refreshed dashboard, improved spending insights and easier card controls. Most members will not need to do anything. We will send detailed instructions two weeks before the change, including the date of a short planned maintenance window.
