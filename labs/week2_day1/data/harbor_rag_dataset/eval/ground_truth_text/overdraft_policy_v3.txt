Harbor Credit Union Overdraft Policy

Policy number: DEP-OD-001 | Version 3.0 | Effective date: January 1, 2026 | Document owner: Deposit Operations | Approved by: Board Risk Committee, October 21, 2025

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy describes how Harbor Credit Union ("Harbor", "we", "us") handles transactions presented against a consumer checking account when the Available Balance is not sufficient to pay them. It applies to all Retail checking products, including Basic Checking, Premier Checking and Student Checking. Business deposit accounts are governed separately by the Business Account Fee Guide and are outside the scope of this policy.

Harbor pays overdrafts at its discretion. Nothing in this policy creates an obligation to pay any item, and Harbor may decline to pay an item even when a member has previously had overdrafts paid.

2. Definitions

- Available Balance means the ledger balance of the account, less any holds placed on deposits and pending debit card authorizations, plus any pending deposits that have been made available.
- Overdraft means a negative Available Balance created when Harbor pays an item for which funds are not available.
- Courtesy Pay means Harbor's discretionary overdraft service for eligible Retail checking accounts, subject to the limit described in Section 3.
- Overdraft Transfer Protection means an automatic transfer from a linked Primary Share Account (savings) to cover an item that would otherwise overdraw checking.
- Item means any check, ACH debit, recurring debit card payment, bill payment, or other debit presented against the account.

3. Courtesy Pay Eligibility and Limits

A checking account becomes eligible for Courtesy Pay once it has been open for at least 60 days, the primary owner is at least 18 years of age, and the account has received recurring deposits totalling at least $250 in each of the two most recent calendar months. Eligible accounts receive a standard Courtesy Pay limit of $600, which may be reduced or suspended if the account is not brought to a positive balance within the timeframe in Section 7.

The Courtesy Pay limit includes the amount of the overdraft and any fees charged. Members may view their current limit in online banking under Account Services.

4. Overdraft Fees

Harbor charges a fee of $29 per item, up to a maximum of three (3) items per business day, for items paid through Courtesy Pay. No overdraft fee will be charged if the account is overdrawn by $25.00 or less at the end of the business day, and no fee is charged for items that Harbor returns unpaid under this policy. Returned item (NSF) fees are listed in the Schedule of Fees and Charges.

Overdraft Transfer Protection transfers from a linked Primary Share Account carry a transfer fee of $5.00 per transfer. Only one transfer fee is assessed per business day regardless of how many items the transfer covers.

5. ATM and One-Time Debit Card Transactions

Consistent with Regulation E, Harbor will not pay ATM withdrawals or one-time debit card transactions into overdraft under Courtesy Pay unless the member has affirmatively opted in. Members may opt in or revoke their consent at any time at a branch, through the Member Contact Centre, or in online banking. Recurring debit card payments, checks and ACH debits are not affected by this election.

6. Order of Posting

Deposits are credited first. Debit card and ATM transactions are then posted in the order in which they were authorized, followed by checks in check-number order and ACH debits in the order received. Harbor does not re-order transactions to increase the number of overdraft fees.

7. Repayment

Members are expected to return an overdrawn account to a positive balance promptly. If an account remains overdrawn for 35 consecutive days, Courtesy Pay is suspended. If an account remains overdrawn for 60 consecutive days, it may be closed and the negative balance referred for recovery.

8. Member Notices

Harbor sends an overdraft notice by the member's selected delivery method (e-mail, text alert or mail) on the business day after an item is paid into overdraft or returned. Monthly and year-to-date totals of overdraft and returned item fees are shown on each periodic statement.

9. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually by Deposit Operations and Compliance. Version 3.0 supersedes Version 2.0 (effective March 1, 2024). Summary of changes: the per-item fee was reduced from $32 to the amount in Section 4, the daily fee cap was lowered, the de minimis threshold was raised, the Overdraft Transfer Protection fee was reduced and is now charged once per day, the standard Courtesy Pay limit was increased, and the eligibility waiting period was shortened.
