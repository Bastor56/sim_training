Business Account Fee Guide

Effective date: January 1, 2026 | Applies to: Business Services members | Owner: Business Services

This guide lists the fees and limits that apply to Harbor Credit Union business deposit accounts. Consumer (Retail) accounts are governed by the Retail Schedule of Fees and Charges, the Overdraft Policy and the Wire Transfer Policy, which contain different amounts.

Business Checking Products

Business Essentials Checking has a monthly maintenance fee of $10.00, waived with an average daily balance of $2,500 or more. It includes 200 free transactions per statement cycle, after which each debit or deposited item is charged $0.40.

Business Analysis Checking is designed for higher-volume businesses. Balances in Business Analysis Checking earn an Earnings Credit Rate (ECR) of 0.35%, which offsets monthly service charges. Service charges not covered by the earnings credit are debited from the account after the end of each month. The monthly account maintenance fee is $15.00.

Business Overdraft

Business overdraft items are charged $35.00 each, up to five (5) per business day. Business accounts are not eligible for Courtesy Pay; overdrafts are paid only under an approved Business Overdraft Line of Credit or at Harbor's discretion. Transfers from a linked business savings account to cover an overdraft are charged $12.00 per transfer.

Business Wire Transfers

Outgoing domestic wires from Business accounts are charged $20.00 per wire. Outgoing international wires from Business accounts are charged $40.00 per wire. Incoming wires to Business accounts are charged $10.00 per wire. The same-day cutoff for business domestic wires submitted through Business Online is 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time. Business wires above $100,000 require dual approval by two authorized signers.

Business Remote and Mobile Deposit

The standard Business mobile deposit limit is $25,000 per business day and $100,000 per rolling 30 days. Remote deposit capture with a desktop scanner is available for a monthly fee of $30.00 and carries limits set during underwriting.

Business Debit Cards

Business debit cards have a daily ATM withdrawal limit of $1,000 and a daily purchase limit of $7,500. Higher limits may be approved by Business Services for established accounts.

Other Business Services

- Stop payment order: $35.00 per request.
- Cash handling (currency deposited over $10,000 per month): $0.15 per $100.
- Night depository bags: $25.00 per bag set.
- ACH origination: $25.00 per month plus $0.10 per entry.
- Positive Pay: $40.00 per month.

Fees are subject to change with 30 days' written notice. Business members should contact their Business Relationship Manager with questions about pricing or earnings credit calculations.
