Savings Account Terms and Conditions

Form DEP-TC-003 | Revised January 1, 2026

1. Membership and Par Value

To become a member of Harbor Credit Union you must meet the field of membership requirements in our bylaws and purchase and maintain the par value of one share in a Primary Share Account. The par value of a share is $5.00. If the balance of your Primary Share Account falls below the par value, your membership may be terminated after written notice.

2. Dividends

Dividends are paid from current income and available earnings after required transfers to reserves. The dividend rate and Annual Percentage Yield on savings accounts are variable and may change at any time as determined by the Board of Directors. Dividends are compounded daily and credited monthly for the Primary Share Account, Youth Savings and Money Market accounts.

You must maintain a minimum daily balance of $100 in your Primary Share Account to earn dividends. If the daily balance is below this amount on any day, no dividends are earned for that day. Harbor uses the daily balance method, which applies a daily periodic rate to the principal in the account each day.

3. Dividend Period

The Dividend Period is monthly. For example, the beginning date of the first dividend period of the calendar year is January 1 and the ending date is January 31. All other dividend periods follow the same pattern. If you close your account before dividends are credited, you will receive the accrued dividends through the date of closure.

4. Transaction Limitations

You may make an unlimited number of deposits. Harbor limits withdrawals and transfers out of each savings account to six (6) per calendar month by preauthorized, automatic, telephone or online transfer. Transactions in excess of this limit are subject to the excessive withdrawal fee shown below and repeated excess activity may result in the account being converted to a checking account.

5. Fees Applicable to Savings

Fee | Amount
Excessive withdrawal (after six per month) | $10.00 each
Account closed within 90 days of opening | $25.00
Returned deposited item | $15.00 per item
Money Market balance below $2,500 minimum | $8.00 per month

6. Dormant Accounts and Unclaimed Property

An account with no member-initiated activity for twelve months is classified as dormant. Dormant account fees are listed in the Schedule of Fees and Charges. If an account remains inactive for the period set by state law, Harbor is required to transfer the funds to the state as unclaimed property.

7. Deposits and Holds

Funds deposited to savings are available according to the Funds Availability Policy. Harbor may refuse any deposit, limit the amount that may be deposited, or return all or part of a deposit.

8. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the bylaws of Harbor Credit Union, federal law and regulations, and the laws of the state in which the credit union is chartered. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.
