Auto Loan Product Comparison

New, used and refinance loans | Rates as of September 1, 2026

Harbor Credit Union offers new and used vehicle loans and refinancing with fixed rates, no application fee and no prepayment penalty. This sheet compares our auto loan products, current rate ranges by credit tier, and how quickly members receive decisions through each application channel.

Who can apply

Any Harbor member aged 18 or older may apply. Applicants must provide proof of income, a valid driver's license and, for purchases, a buyer's order or purchase agreement. Co-applicants are welcome and can help you qualify for a lower tier.

Vehicles we finance

We finance cars, light trucks, SUVs and vans up to ten model years old with fewer than 125,000 miles. New vehicles are current or prior model year with fewer than 7,500 miles. Private-party purchases are available for used vehicles, with title verification handled by Harbor.

Credit tiers

Your rate is based on your credit tier, the vehicle age, the loan term and the loan-to-value ratio (LTV). Tiers are assigned using your credit score at the time of application; see Table 1 on the next page.

Faster decisions

Decision speed varies by how you apply, as shown in Figure 1. Applications with complete income documents are decided faster in every channel.

Rate discounts

Receive a 0.25% rate discount when you set up automatic payments from a Harbor checking account. Members with a Premier Checking relationship receive an additional 0.10% discount. Discounts cannot reduce the rate below 3.99% APR.

Loan terms

Loan amounts start at $5,000. Terms of 61 to 72 months require a minimum loan amount of $20,000. The first payment may be deferred for up to 60 days; interest accrues during the deferral period.

Protection products

Guaranteed Asset Protection (GAP) and mechanical breakdown protection are optional and are not required to obtain a loan. You may cancel GAP within 60 days for a full refund.

How to apply

Apply in the Harbor mobile app, in online banking, by calling the Member Contact Centre, at any branch, or through a participating dealer. Pre-approvals are valid for 60 days.

Important disclosures

APR = Annual Percentage Rate. Rates are subject to change without notice and depend on creditworthiness, vehicle age, term and LTV. Example: a $25,000 loan for 60 months at 4.49% APR has an estimated monthly payment of $465.94. Tier C loans may require a larger down payment.
